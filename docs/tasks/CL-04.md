## Goal
The pure detectors behind three send-time checks exist and are correct on the plan's mandatory cases: domains that look like a trusted domain (F6), recipients the user never wrote to before (F7), and recipients sitting in the wrong To/CC/BCC field (F8). After this card the real `detectFieldRuleConflicts` replaces its stub in `createCore`; the remaining `CoreApi` methods (`evaluate`, `mergePolicy`, `diffRings`, codec, `suggestRing`) stay on the stub until CL-05.

## Design decisions (lead, reversible until Ready)
- **D1 Punycode decoding:** lookalike needs the Unicode form of `xn--` labels to build skeletons. Add an RFC 3492 decoder next to the encoder in `normalize.ts`, tested by round trip and the RFC vectors. No dependency.
- **D2 Bloom geometry:** two generations of 16,384 bits (2 KB) each, 7 hash functions, capacity 1,500 addresses per generation (rotation when `count` reaches it). The union holds 3,000 addresses at a false-positive rate near 1.06 % (plan: at most 1.2 %). Stored as base64url the two strings take about 5.5 KB of `roamingSettings`, over the plan's 4 KB line but inside the 2 KB reserve of the 32 KB budget (plan §5.4); the storage meter in AG-06 shows it. `HistorySchema` already allows this size.
- **D3 Hash:** the Bloom hashes `salt + address` with a hand-written synchronous SHA-256 (about 50 lines, `hash.ts`), using bytes 0 to 7 as two 32-bit values for double hashing. Reason: `crypto.subtle` is asynchronous and unverified in the JS-only runtime, and a non-cryptographic hash would make the stored bits easier to test against a list of guessed addresses. Verified against `node:crypto` in tests only.
- **D4 Reference set:** domains of the current ring's members, the sender's own domain (internal domain), and the members of every other ring that has not expired (same expiry rule as CL-03 D3). Without a ring (FR-6.5) the first two collapse to the internal domain plus the other rings. Matching is on the registrable domain, so `mail.abccorp.vn` is exact for `abccorp.vn` and is never flagged.
- **D5 Scope boundary:** this card returns raw detections (`LookalikeMatch`, `FirstTimeRecipient`, `FieldRuleViolation`). Turning them into `Finding`s with policy modes and i18n messages is CL-05; recording into the filter at send time is CL-08.

## Requirements
1. **`hash`:** `sha256(bytes)`, `utf8(text)` (no `TextEncoder` dependency), both pure.
2. **`lookalike`:** `registrable(domain)` splits a normalised domain into primary label and suffix; the suffix is the last label, or the last two when the last is a two-letter country code and the one before it is a generic second level (`com net org gov edu ac co or ne go`). `skeleton(label)`: decode punycode, NFKC, lower case, map the confusable table (Cyrillic, Greek and other Latin look-alikes, `0` to `o`, `1` to `l`), then `rn` to `m` and `vv` to `w`. `findLookalikes(input)` where input holds the snapshot, the ring (or null), all rings, settings exceptions and `now`; for every recipient whose registrable domain is not itself a reference, report the first reference that matches by priority: (a) skeleton equal but labels different, (b) equal after removing hyphens but different, (c) same primary label with a different suffix, (d) Damerau-Levenshtein (optimal string alignment) at most 1 when the longer label has at most 8 characters, at most 2 otherwise, both labels at least 4 characters. Result rows carry the field, entry, candidate domain, reference domain, reference source (`ring | internal | otherRing`), reference ring id and the rule. User exceptions (unordered pair of registrable domains) suppress a row.
3. **`history`:** `emptyHistory(salt)`, `mightContain(state, address)` (current or previous), `recordAddresses(state, addresses)` (normalises, skips invalid and already-present addresses, rotates at capacity: `previous = current`, new empty `current`, `count = 0`), `parseKnownAddresses(text)` for the pasted list of FR-7.3 (split on whitespace, comma, semicolon; normalise; dedupe; at most 3,000), `historyStats(state)` (count, capacity, estimated false-positive rate). Output always parses under `HistorySchema`; base64url is hand-coded.
4. **First-time detection:** `findFirstTimeRecipients({ ring, snapshot, history, settings, now })`: a recipient is first-time when it is not in the filter and not declared as an `address` member of the ring (domain members do not count, FR-7.2). Returns nothing when `ring.firstTimeCheck` is false (FR-7.6) or while `settings.learning.enabled` holds and `now` is before `learning.until` (FR-7.3). Bloom false positives only ever hide a warning, never raise one.
5. **`fieldRules`:** `evaluateFieldRules(rules, snapshot)` with patterns in the CL-03 grammar (address, domain, `*.domain`; `*` and unparseable patterns are ignored). `mustBeIn X`: violated when no recipient matches in X (absent altogether, or present only in another field, reported with `foundIn`); `mustNotBeIn X`: one violation per matching recipient in X; `onlyIn X`: one violation per matching recipient outside X. `when: "anyExternal"` applies a rule only if some recipient (unresolved ones count as external) is outside the sender's domain. Never moves anyone (FR-8.3).
6. **Conflicts (FR-8.4):** `detectFieldRuleConflicts` reports pairs whose patterns overlap (equal, or one covers the other, reusing CL-03 `covers`) and that cannot both hold: `mustBeIn X` with `mustNotBeIn X`; `mustBeIn X` with `onlyIn Y` for Y not X; `mustNotBeIn X` with `onlyIn X`; `onlyIn X` with `onlyIn Y` for Y not X. Each pair once, in rule order.
7. **`createCore`:** also replaces `detectFieldRuleConflicts` with the real function. Signatures unchanged.
8. **Performance (NFR-1):** 50 recipients, a 500-member ring and ten other 500-member rings: lookalike, field rules and history together best of five runs under 50 ms.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] AC-1 Lookalike mandatory cases as named tests: `abcc0rp.vn`, `abccorp.com`, `abc-corp.vn` and the Cyrillic `аbccorp.vn` (as `xn--` form) are flagged against `abccorp.vn`; `abccorp.vn`, `mail.abccorp.vn` and an unrelated domain are not; exceptions suppress in both orders; expired rings are not references; works with no ring; each row names the reference domain, source and ring.
- [ ] AC-2 `punycodeDecode(punycodeEncode(x)) = x` for random labels (property) and on the RFC 3492 vectors.
- [ ] AC-3 Bloom: false-positive rate at most 1.2 % with 3,000 distinct inserted addresses (fixed seed, at least 20,000 probes); no false negatives inside the two-generation window (property); rotation keeps the previous generation and drops the older; state round-trips and parses under `HistorySchema`; the encoded state does not contain any inserted address; a different salt gives different bits.
- [ ] AC-4 `sha256` equals `node:crypto` on the NIST vectors and on random byte strings (property); `utf8` equals `TextEncoder` on random strings.
- [ ] AC-5 First-time detection table: address member known, domain member not known, learning window on and off, ring switch off, filter hit, import list parsing and caps, recording makes the next check silent.
- [ ] AC-6 Field-rule table: each of the three types with recipient absent, present in the right field and present in the wrong field; `anyExternal` true and false; unresolved recipient counts as external; `*` and junk patterns ignored; no recipient is ever moved.
- [ ] AC-7 Conflict table: the four conflicting shapes (with equal and with covering patterns) and non-conflicts (different patterns, `mustBeIn` in two fields, `mustBeIn X` with `onlyIn X`); `core.detectFieldRuleConflicts` is the real function; contract tests pass unchanged.
- [ ] AC-8 Coverage on `packages/core` at least 90 %, mutation score at least 80 % (both reported in the PR), `npm run verify` green, no dependency added, no `Office.*` or DOM type in core.
- [ ] AC-9 The performance test of requirement 8 passes.

## Edge cases
- Single-label domains, IP-literal domains, punycode labels that fail to decode (treated as opaque labels), very long labels.
- Recipients with the same domain repeated: the comparison is cached per domain, rows stay per recipient.
- A ring whose own members already contain two look-alike domains: both are references, neither is flagged against the other.
- Empty reference set, empty rule list, empty filter, filter state with an invalid base64url string (treated as empty, never throws).
- Address casing and `+tag` handling follow CL-03 normalisation.
